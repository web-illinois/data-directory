Edit Office General Information

Summary

Update your office's name, description, contact information, and links.

Path: Office Administration > General Information

Requires office administrator access.

Steps

  1. Open General Information from the left menu.
  2. If you administer more than one office, choose the office first.
  3. Update the fields below.
  4. Select Save.

Fields

  • Office Name (required)
  • Office Type
  • Office Description - a brief blurb about your office. Shows in the internal directory.
  • Who does this office serve? - for example, students in general, staff, or students of a particular college. If your office is listed, this appears on the Tech Help Center Help Desks page.

Contact Information

  • Email - use a department or unit email. Do not use an individual's email address.
  • Phone - use a department or unit number, including area code. Do not use an individual's number.
  • External Url - the link outside users will use to reach your site. Use Test External Link to verify it.

Additional location and display fields appear lower on the page.
