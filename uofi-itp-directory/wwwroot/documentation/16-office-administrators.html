Manage Office Administrators

Summary

Control who can edit your office.

Path: Office Administration > Office Administrators

What administrators can do

Office administrators can edit office information, create other office administrators for their office, and edit the people in their office.

Administrator types

  • Primary Administrators - visible to the outside world.
  • Backup Administrators - not visible to the outside world, but have the same permissions as a primary administrator.

Add an administrator

  1. Open Office Administrators.
  2. Enter the person's Net ID under Add a New Administrator. The @illinois.edu suffix is optional.
  3. Select Save.

Change or remove an administrator

Use the controls next to the person's name in the Primary or Backup lists to change their type or remove them.
