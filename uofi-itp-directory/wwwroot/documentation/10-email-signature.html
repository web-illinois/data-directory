Generate an Email Signature

Summary

Create an email signature from your profile information to paste into Outlook.

Path: Profile Administration > Generate Email Signature

Steps

  1. Open Generate Email Signature from the left menu.
  2. Select Generate Signature. The signature opens in a new tab.
  3. Copy the signature.
  4. Paste it into your Outlook signature settings.

Tip

The signature uses your profile information. Update your general information and job information first so the signature is accurate.
