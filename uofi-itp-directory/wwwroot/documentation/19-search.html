Find an Administrator or Profile

Summary

Use the search pages to find who to contact when information is incorrect, or to look up a profile.

Find an administrator

If you find incorrect information about an office or unit, find its administrators and their contact information.

  1. From the home page, use the Find an Administrator card.
  2. Select Office Admins or Unit Admins.
  3. Choose the office or unit to see its administrators.

Search for a profile

Path: Search > People

  1. Type a Net ID in Lookup / Filter Net ID.
  2. Choose a profile from the list.
  3. Select View / Edit Profile.

This searches only people inside the WIGG Directory. To search the entire campus, use the campus directory.

Logs and change history

Search also includes pages for logs and change history. For example, after removing a tag you can check the logs to see if the change has been processed.
