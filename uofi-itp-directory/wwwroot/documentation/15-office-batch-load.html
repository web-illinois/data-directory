Batch Load People to Your Office

Summary

Add several people at once when they share the same job title and job type.

Path: Office Administration > Batch Load People to Your Office

Requires office administrator access.

Steps

  1. Open Batch Load People to Your Office.
  2. In Net IDs, enter one Net ID per line. The @illinois.edu suffix is optional.
  3. Enter the Title that applies to everyone in the batch.
  4. Choose a Job Type.
  5. Select Add Employees.
  6. Review the Results shown below the button.

Notes

  • Everyone in a batch gets the same title and job type. Edit individuals afterward if needed (see Add, Edit, and Remove People in Your Office).
  • A job type of None means the person will not be listed.
  • People will be added to your profile on the next run unless you specifically mark them as None.
