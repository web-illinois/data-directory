Add, Edit, and Remove People in Your Office

Summary

Manage the list of people connected to your office.

Path: Office Administration > Edit People in Your Office

Requires office administrator access.

Edit a person

  1. Open Edit People in Your Office.
  2. Choose a person from the People in Your Office list.
  3. Select Edit Employee to update that person's profile.

Add a person

  1. Enter the person's Net ID in Add a New Person. The @illinois.edu suffix is optional.
  2. Select Add Employee.

To add many people at once, see Batch Load People to Your Office.

Remove a person

  1. Choose the person from the list.
  2. Select Remove Profile.

Removing a person only removes their connection to your office. The employee's information is not deleted.

Clear the cache

Use the Clear Cache button after making changes if updates do not appear right away.
