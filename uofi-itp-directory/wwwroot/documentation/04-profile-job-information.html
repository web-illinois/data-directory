Edit Your Job / Position Information

Summary

The Job / Position Information page lets you set your title and job type for each office you belong to.

Path: Profile Administration > Job / Position Information

Not every field on this page may be editable by you. Some are controlled by an office administrator.

Steps

  1. Open Job / Position Information from the left menu.
  2. For each office listed, update the fields below.
  3. Select Save.

Fields

  • Title - your job title for that office.
  • Job Type - the category for your role in that office. A job type of None means you will not be listed.
  • Job-specific Description - shown only when the office uses descriptions. Used for office-specific directories.

Multiple offices

If you belong to more than one office, a Primary Office selector appears at the top.

  • Your primary office determines the title shown when someone views your profile.
  • If you do not choose one, the first office created is used.
  • The primary office is marked (primary) in the page headings.
