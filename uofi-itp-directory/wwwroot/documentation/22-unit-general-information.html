Edit Unit General Information

Summary

Update your unit's name, type, audience, and web links.

Path: Unit Administration > General Information

Requires unit administrator access.

Steps

  1. Open General Information from the left menu.
  2. If you administer more than one unit, choose the unit first.
  3. Update the fields below.
  4. Select Save.

Fields

  • Unit Name (required)
  • Unit Type - choose the type that best describes your unit.
  • Audience - who does this unit serve? For example, students in general, staff, or students of a particular college.

Contact Information

  • External Url - the link outside users will use to access your site. Use Test External Link to verify it.
  • Internal Url - the link users inside the University of Illinois should use. If it is the same as your external link, you may leave this blank. Use Test Internal Link to verify it.

Notes

  • Use Clear Cache after saving if your changes do not appear right away.
  • If you arrived from a search page, a Back to Searching for Units button is shown at the top.
