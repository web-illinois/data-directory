# Getting Started and Signing In

## Summary

The WIGG Directory lets faculty and staff enter and maintain directory information for units, offices, and individual profiles.

## Who can use it

Anyone with an Illinois account can sign in. What you see depends on your role:

| Role | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Employee with a profile | Edit your own profile, preview it, and generate an email signature. |
| Office administrator | Edit office information, office hours, and the people in your office. |
| Unit administrator | Everything an office administrator can do, plus add and remove offices, manage unit administrators, internal information, and tags. |

## The home page

After signing in you will see cards for the tasks available to you:

- **Edit My Profile** - shown if you have a profile. Choose **Edit** to change it, or use the preview button to see how it looks.
- **Office Administration** - shown if you are an administrator. Shortcuts for **Edit People** and **Edit Office Hours**.
- **Find an Administrator** - available to everyone. Look up office or unit administrators if you find incorrect information.

## Navigation

When you open a section, a menu appears on the left side of the page:

- **Profile Administration** - your profile pages.
- **Office Administration** - your office pages.
- **Unit Administration** - your unit pages.

## Information managed elsewhere

Some information (such as your name and address) can also be edited in [My Profile in My UI Info](https://go.uillinois.edu/myprofile). Changes made there are copied to the directory, but this can take a few days. See [Edit Your General Profile Information](03-profile-general-information.md).

## Unsaved changes

Most pages warn you if you try to leave with unsaved changes. Always select **Save** before leaving a page.
