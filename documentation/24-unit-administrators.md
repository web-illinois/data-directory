# Manage Unit Administrators

## Summary

Control who can administer your unit.

**Path:** Unit Administration > Unit Administrators

*Requires unit administrator access.*

## What unit administrators can do

Unit administrators can create new offices, create other unit administrators for their unit, and create office administrators.

## Administrator types

- **Primary Administrators** - visible to the outside world.
- **Backup Administrators** - not visible to the outside world, but have the same permissions as a primary administrator.

## Add an administrator

1. Open **Unit Administrators**. If you administer more than one unit, choose the unit first.
2. Enter the person's Net ID under **Add a New Unit Administrator**. The `@illinois.edu` suffix is optional.
3. Select **Save**.

## Change or remove an administrator

Use the controls next to the person's name in the Primary or Backup lists to change their type or remove them.

## Notes

Use **Clear Cache** after saving if your changes do not appear right away.
