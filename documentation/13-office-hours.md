# Set Office Hours

## Summary

Set the hours your office is open.

**Path:** Office Administration > Office Hours

*Requires office administrator access.*

## Steps

1. Open **Office Hours** from the left menu.
2. For each day, enter the opening and closing time.
3. Optionally choose a location type for the day (Blank, On Campus, Remote, or Other with a note).
4. Optionally select **Add the text "Closed for University Holidays" to the office hours**.
5. Select **Save**.

## Rules

- If either the opening or closing time is blank, that day will **not** be displayed.
- Use the format `8:00 AM`.
- If your office is open 24 hours, enter `12:00 AM` for **both** the start and end times.
